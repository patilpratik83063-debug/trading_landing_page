Quick answers

Where is my receipt?

Receipts are emailed within minutes of a successful payment. Check spam, then contact us with the email used at checkout.

Payment failed — was I charged?

A failed or cancelled checkout should not create a charge. Pending authorisations are usually released by your bank automatically.

Do you offer signals?

No. Demonfx teaches analysis, risk and process. We do not sell signals, copy trading or managed accounts.
