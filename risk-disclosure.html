Risk Disclosure

Read before enrolling Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]
Forex trading can lose you money. A large proportion of retail trading accounts lose money over time. Do not trade with funds you cannot afford to lose, and do not assume that education removes the risk of loss.

1. Educational scope

Demonfx is an education provider. We teach concepts, analysis methods and risk practices. We do not provide investment advice, personal recommendations, portfolio management, brokerage services, signals or copy trading. Nothing on this website is a solicitation to trade any instrument or to open an account with any provider.

2. No guarantee of results

No educator, system or strategy can guarantee profit. Any example, illustration, chart annotation or case study on this site is provided to explain a concept and is not a projection of your results. Past performance — yours or anyone else's — does not indicate future performance.

3. Leverage and amplified loss

Forex is commonly traded with leverage. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses and can result in losses exceeding your initial deposit, depending on the provider's terms and negative-balance protections. A small adverse price movement can eliminate a large portion of an account when position size is not controlled.

4. Market risk and volatility

Currency prices are affected by central-bank policy, interest-rate expectations, inflation data, employment figures, geopolitical events, liquidity conditions and sentiment. Gaps can occur between the price at which you expect to exit and the price you receive. Stops are not guarantees of execution at a specific level.

5. Execution, slippage and counterparty risk

Spreads widen around news and session opens. Orders may be filled with slippage, rejected or partially filled. If you trade with a broker or platform, you take on that provider's counterparty, custody and insolvency risk. Demonfx does not endorse or supervise any broker and cannot recover funds held by a third party.

6. Technology and access risk

Platform outages, connectivity failures, device problems and errors in any trading tool can prevent you from managing a position. Always have a plan for what happens if you cannot access your account during a fast market.

7. Psychological and behavioural risk

Fear, greed, boredom, overconfidence after wins and revenge after losses lead to rule-breaking. The course teaches routines intended to reduce these behaviours, but you remain responsible for your decisions. If trading is affecting your wellbeing, stop and seek appropriate support.

8. Costs reduce returns

Spreads, commissions, overnight financing, conversion fees and taxes reduce any gross result. A strategy that appears profitable in a backtest may be unprofitable after realistic costs and slippage.

9. Suitability

Trading is not suitable for everyone. Consider your financial situation, objectives, experience and tolerance for loss — and seek independent licensed advice if you are unsure. Do not borrow money to trade, and do not use funds needed for living expenses or debt repayment.

Demonfx is an education business and does not hold itself out as a licensed investment adviser, broker or fund manager.
