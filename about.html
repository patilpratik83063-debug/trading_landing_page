About

An education academy for people who take the market seriously.

Demonfx exists for a simple reason: most Forex beginners are taught fragments — a setup here, an indicator there — and never given a structured way to think about price, risk or their own behaviour.

What we teach

The Forex Trading Masterclass is built as a sequence: market fundamentals, price reading, technical context, key levels, strategy and backtesting, then risk and psychology. Each module assumes only what the previous module taught, so the learning compounds instead of accumulating into noise.

Every concept is demonstrated on annotated charts, including examples where the idea fails. Students are asked to write plans, size positions and keep a journal — because those habits, not prediction, are what a repeatable process depends on.

What we deliberately do not do

  • We do not sell trading signals, copy trades or manage client capital.
  • We do not recommend brokers, funded-account programmes or specific instruments.
  • We do not promise profits, income or a fixed success rate.
  • We do not publish testimonials, student counts or ratings we cannot verify.
  • We do not provide personal investment, tax or legal advice.

How we describe results

Trading outcomes depend on the individual, the market and risk decisions no educator controls. We describe what the curriculum is designed to teach, not what a student will earn. Where verified student feedback is available and the student has given written permission, it will be published in their own words without editing the substance.

Editorial and content standards

  • Course examples use historical, annotated charts clearly labelled as illustrations.
  • Risk concepts are taught before any strategy application.
  • Placeholders on this site are marked so nothing unverified is presented as fact.
  • Material corrections are made when content becomes inaccurate, with the update date recorded.
