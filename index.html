01The starting point

Stop Trading on Guesswork.

Most beginners do not lose money because the market is rigged. They lose it because they enter without a process — no defined risk, no reviewed decisions and no way to tell luck from skill.

What unstructured trading looks like

  • Capital eroded by oversized positions, one trade at a time.
  • Decisions driven by screen time and social media, not evidence.
  • No written record, so the same mistake repeats unnoticed.
  • Confidence that swings with the last trade instead of the process.
  1. 01

    Entering trades without a clear strategy

    If you cannot state your entry, invalidation and target before you click, you are not trading a plan — you are reacting to a chart.

  2. 02

    Misunderstanding technical indicators

    Indicators are derived from price. Used without context they add delay and false confidence, not clarity.

  3. 03

    Taking excessive risks

    Position size is usually decided by feeling, or by the maximum the platform allows — never by the distance to invalidation.

  4. 04

    Making emotional trading decisions

    Fear closes winners early. Hope keeps losers open. Both are predictable responses to unmanaged risk.

  5. 05

    Lacking a structured learning process

    Random videos and borrowed setups create a patchwork of ideas, not a repeatable framework you can test and improve.

Every part of the programme builds on the last — from how the market actually works to how you document decisions so you can improve them.

What a currency pair really represents, who moves price, how sessions and liquidity shape spreads, and why the economic calendar matters.

AnalysisModule 03

Support and Resistance

Draw levels that survive contact with price, identify supply and demand zones, and use confluence without turning your chart into art.

Backtesting and Trading Journals
